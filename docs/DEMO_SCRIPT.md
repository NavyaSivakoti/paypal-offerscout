# OfferScout demo recording plan

Target length: 2 minutes 30 seconds. Record after the live model and PayPal sandbox are configured and verified. Show the actual running application. Use original narration and screen recording; no background music or third-party retailer imagery is needed.

| Time | Screen action | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Start on Home, select Savings, and show merchant cards and the sample-data notice. | “Finding a cashback offer is only the beginning. Caps, minimums, and shipping can change what you save. OfferScout helps shoppers compare those details before paying. This demo uses fictional offers.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Open Ask OfferScout at bottom right. Expand Demo settings & eligibility, select Live AI, then collapse settings. Ask “Running shoes under $120.” Show the returned model and three matches. | “I describe what I need. AI turns that request into structured filters. The app keeps the payment within my budget and compares estimated value.” |
| 0:45–1:10 | Open the Stride Supply chat result, close details, then open the Trail & Field result. Show each sample purchase and its terms. | “Fifteen percent sounds best, but this offer caps cashback at five dollars. Its estimated cost is $105. Ten percent uncapped on a $108 shoe gives an estimated cost of $97.20. The lower rate wins.” |
| 1:10–1:55 | Choose Trail & Field. Continue to PayPal sandbox, approve as the test buyer, then complete capture. Show the confirmed order/capture IDs. | “I still approve the payment. The server charges $108, not the estimated cost after rewards. PayPal sandbox confirms the test payment. No real money moves and no cashback is issued.” |
| 1:55–2:15 | Return to Home and reopen Ask OfferScout. Re-select Live AI if navigation reset the page. Ask “Ridgeline Trail Shoe at Everyday Athletics.” Leave customer status unknown and inspect terms. | “When eligibility is unknown, we show it. This new-customer-only offer gets no estimated reward until the shopper declares eligibility. That declaration is not verified externally.” |
| 2:15–2:30 | Return to the home page. | “OfferScout: your AI guide to cashback offers. AI interprets the request, code checks the math, and PayPal powers the sandbox checkout. A real offer feed is a future integration.” |

## Recording checks

- Record the real live-mode run. Do not label rule-based demo answers as AI results.
- Keep all sample-data and sandbox labels visible. Never show API secrets or buyer passwords.
- Verify a COMPLETED capture before recording a success claim; do not substitute a mocked receipt.
- Login transitions may be cut for privacy and timing. Do not fabricate outcomes or conceal failed steps.
- Confirm final length is below three minutes, upload publicly to YouTube, and verify signed-out playback.
- If the actual flow takes longer, shorten the introduction and closing; keep the offer comparison and confirmed transaction.
